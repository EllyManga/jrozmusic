About the Artist

Born to
Command
the Stage

J. Roz is one of Kenya's most exciting emerging music forces — a performing artist whose sound sits at the intersection of Afro-fusion, R&B, and the raw energy of Nairobi street culture. Her music doesn't just play. It moves rooms.

With a growing digital following and a reputation for commanding live performances, J. Roz has established herself as a youth culture powerhouse. She speaks the language of Kenya's next generation — authentically, boldly, and without compromise.

For brands, event organizers, and media professionals, J. Roz represents more than an artist. She is an influence vehicle — a direct line to the youth audience that makes purchasing decisions, drives trends, and defines culture.
